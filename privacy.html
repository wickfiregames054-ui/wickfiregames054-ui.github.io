Ember Horde Privacy Policy

Wickfire Games · Last updated 3 October 2026

Ember Horde is a mobile game published on Google Play by Wickfire Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information we collect

We don't ask for your name, email or any account. The game has no sign-in and we run no servers that receive your data.

Your progress (gold, upgrades, best time and settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it, unless your phone restores it from an Android backup.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this as described in its policy on how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  • Buying "Remove ads" turns off the ads that play between runs. Optional video ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google tells the game only whether you own "Remove ads".

Children

Ember Horde is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
